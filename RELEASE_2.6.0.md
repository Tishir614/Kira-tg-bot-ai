# KiraBot 2.6.0

## Added
- `/memory` (память), `/memory add <текст>`, `/forget` (забыть): view, add and wipe what Kira remembers about the user.
- RP library and tools: `/rp save|load|list` (character cards per user, max 30), `/rp roll 2d6+1` (dice result is woven into the story), `/rp random` (random scene), `/rp export` (scene as a .txt file).
- Image styles: `/image <описание>, стиль:киберпанк` (12 presets, `стиль:рандом`), `/styles`.

## Untouched
- The masturbate and alcohol modules (`bot/handlers/masturbate/*`, `bot/handlers/alcohol/*`) were not modified.
