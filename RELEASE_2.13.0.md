# KiraBot 2.13.0 Fursona & Formats

- `/fursona <описание>` — save your character's look once; `/fursona` shows it, `/fursona clear` removes it, `/fursona rp` sets it as your RP character. `/me_image <сцена>` (я_картинка) draws it in any scene; works with `стиль:` and `формат:`. Descriptions mentioning minors/ages under 18 are rejected.
- Image format option for `/image` and `/me_image`: `формат:портрет` (512×768), `формат:квадрат` (512×512), `формат:широкий` (768×512). Sent to AI Horde and Perchance as real sizes (ComfyUI keeps its configured size).
- `/styles` lists styles and formats.
- masturbate and alcohol modules untouched.
